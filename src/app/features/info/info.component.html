About LifeOS

LifeOS is a personal finance app that helps you organize your everyday money: track income and expenses, see your spending habits, and plan with built-in calculators. Optional AI Insights can answer questions about your finances in plain language.

App information & privacy

What stays on your device, and what can leave it.

  • Stored on this device

    Your transactions and settings are saved locally in this browser (or the LifeOS app) using IndexedDB. LifeOS has no accounts and no server database.

  • No server needed for your data

    Because your data lives on this device, transactions, the dashboard, and the calculators don't depend on a LifeOS server. The Android app works without internet. Only AI features always need a connection.

  • No permissions required

    LifeOS does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts, or notification access. Files you attach are chosen by you through your device's file picker.

  • Not auto-synced

    Data is not synchronized to other devices. Clearing your browser or app data may remove it — use Export in Settings to keep a copy.

  • Cloud sync (future)

    Optional cloud synchronization may be added in a future version. Today there is no sync.

  • ✦

    When you use AI

    Nothing goes to an AI provider unless you connect one and use an AI feature. When you do, LifeOS sends your question, a summary of figures it calculated, and any files you attach, through the LifeOS AI service to your chosen provider.

How AI Insights works

Ask a question on the AI Insights page, such as "Where did I spend the most this month?". LifeOS adds a summary of figures it has already calculated from your data, and your AI provider writes an answer in plain language.

All calculations stay with LifeOS. Totals, savings, EMI, PF, tax, and take-home pay come from LifeOS's own calculators, and AI is told to quote them, not work out its own. AI can explain and suggest; it isn't professional financial, tax, or legal advice.

You can attach images and PDFs (such as a payslip) to a question. The same AI connection also powers the salary-document reader in the Income Tax Calculator.

Privacy when using AI

Sent when you ask a question:

  • Your question and the earlier messages of the current conversation.
  • Totals LifeOS calculated: income, expenses and savings per month, category totals, a six-month trend, and the dates, categories and amounts of your five largest recent expenses.
  • Your Income Tax Calculator result, if you calculated one in this session.
  • Any images or PDFs you attach.

Never sent: transaction descriptions, notes, payment methods, or your full transaction list.

Your API key: checked with your provider once, then stored only in encrypted form; LifeOS shows just its last four characters. Disconnect any time to remove it from this device.

Your AI provider handles what you send under its own terms and privacy policy. Avoid attaching documents with details you don't want to share, such as PAN, Aadhaar, or account numbers.

Troubleshooting AI

"We couldn't authenticate with your AI provider"
The key is wrong, was deleted, or was copied incompletely. Create a new key and use Change Key above.
"No AI credit available"
Your provider account needs API credit. A ChatGPT or Claude subscription doesn't include API usage — check billing on the provider's API site.
"You're offline"
AI needs internet. Reconnect, then tap Try Again — your question is kept.
"Too many requests" or "That took too long"
Wait a moment and try again. Shorter questions and smaller files are faster.
"AI isn't available here yet" or "There's an issue connecting to AI"
The LifeOS AI service isn't reachable from this version of the app. Everything else in LifeOS keeps working.
