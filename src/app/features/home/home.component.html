Your data stays on your device

Local Storage

V1 stores all data locally in your browser using IndexedDB. Nothing is sent to a server.

No Permissions Required

LifeOS does not request microphone, camera, location, contacts, or notification access.

Not Auto-Synced

Data is not automatically synchronized to other devices. Clearing browser data may remove it.

Cloud Sync (Future)

Optional cloud synchronization can be added in a future version. For now, everything is local.
